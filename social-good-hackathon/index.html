What is the Social Good Hackathon?

A one-day hackathon where teams build a minimum viable product (MVP) that helps power social good, using one of our ideas or one of their own.

  • Apply as an individual or as a team
  • Take one of our ideas or bring your own
  • Your case study brief arrives about a week before the day, so you come in ready
  • On the day, you build with your team, then you present what you made to the judges
  • The best teams mix different skills, like a designer, a programmer and a business person
  • Any work experience, skill, or niche idea is welcome, we're ready to hear your idea!
  • Transport provided
  • Lunch provided
  • A coworking space in Makati

All you need to do is show up and give your best work.

Who can join

Who should apply?

Anyone interested in entrepreneurship, including college students.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Teams mix skills, and a team without a developer can win.

Do I need a team or an idea before I apply?

No. We're happy to pair you with a team, and we can give you one of our ideas.

I work for Foundry for Good. Can I join?

No, Foundry for Good employees can't join this hackathon.

Getting there

Where is the hackathon?

We'll post the venue name and address here as soon as it's booked.

How does transport work?

We pay your actual round-trip fare, up to ₱1,500, by GCash or bank transfer. Send us your receipts or app screenshots after the event. If you have no receipt for a jeepney or bus ride, tell us the fare and we'll pay up to ₱400.

What should I bring?

Your laptop, your charger and a valid ID for the building's front desk. We'll take care of lunch and Wi-Fi.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. It's free.

Your idea

Can our customers be outside the Philippines?

Yes. Your customers can be nonprofits in the Philippines, the US or anywhere.

Prizes and ownership

If my team wins, do I have to start a business with Foundry for Good?

No. Joining doesn't commit anyone to start a business with us.

Who owns what my team builds?

Your team keeps ownership of your own original ideas. The ideas on this page are Foundry for Good's ideas, and Foundry for Good may keep developing any of them, with anyone, after the event.
