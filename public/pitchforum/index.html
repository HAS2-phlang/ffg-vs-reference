The pitch experience

  • Who you're talking to: our panel is made up of industry experts and operators in the space. They have built, run, and successfully sold a company that serves nonprofits, schools, or associations. They will weigh your business on what it sells, to whom, and how fast it is growing.
  • How it runs: we host these sessions quarterly on Zoom. You'll be scheduled your own dedicated time slot to pitch and we'll give you our feedback.
  • What comes next: after your session, an investor might offer to fund you, partner with you, or mentor you to help prepare for a later round.

What happens if we invest

If we decide to partner up, we bring a lot more to the table than just capital to help you grow:

  • Context and knowledge: deep expertise on how to effectively build for and sell to the nonprofit sector.
  • The right network: direct introductions to specialized partners, nonprofits, and resources tailored to this market.
  • Targeted reach: access to niche marketing channels that put your product directly in front of nonprofits.
Eligibility

Who can apply.

We back B2B companies selling to nonprofits, associations, foundations, schools and social enterprises. Within that market we look for two situations, and we evaluate every company against the same four criteria.

Two kinds of company we back

Early stage, pre-seed or seed

You have a working product and traction through paying customers or early revenue. The idea is proven. What you need is to reach the next level faster than you could on your own.

Growth is stable but no longer accelerating

A proven product with real demand and customers, but your growth rate is slowing down, and you want strategic investors who can help take it to the next level.

What we evaluate

All four apply to every company. The strongest candidates speak to each one clearly and honestly.

Integrated purpose and strategy

The mission is woven into the business model, the operations and the day-to-day decisions. Pursuing it is tied to long-term value, not bolted on as marketing.

Measurable impact and evidence

A clear statement of the problem and evidence that your product solves it. Pilot results, customer outcomes and real numbers count for more than projections.

Scalability for good

A model with real room to grow, where the impact grows with it. Show us that profit and purpose reinforce each other in your case.

The right ask

You know exactly what you need: the capital, the specific introductions, the expertise gaps. Founders who can name the three relationships that would change their trajectory stand out.

Session structure

How each session runs.

Foundry for Good runs the same format for every company.

STEP 01 · 5 TO 10 MINUTES

Your pitch

Six slides: problem, solution, traction, team, gaps, the ask.

STEP 02 · QUESTIONS

Q&A

The investors ask you questions to align on where you want to take your business.

STEP 03 · ON ZOOM

We deliberate

You drop off the call. Each investor discusses and weighs your pitch.

STEP 04 · WITHIN 48 HOURS

We get back to you

In writing with where the investors landed and why.

Investors mainly want to weigh

1

How big is the market?

2

How strong is the product?

3

How is the traction?

4

Can the team in place can pull it off?

What happens next

  • If two or more angels want to proceed: we set up a deeper diligence call with you.
  • If they want to mentor: we set-up an alignment call.
