Scaling through the network

Distribution is the unfair advantage.

A new company's slowest years are usually the ones spent earning an audience. Our ventures skip most of that. Across the Foundry for Good network we own 17 domains and the sector relationships that come with them: content, community, and search footprints that already reach the exact buyers a new venture needs.

Those channels are real and named. Nexus Marketing has ranked nonprofit-sector content for over a decade, NXUnite convenes thousands of nonprofit professionals, and Double the Donation sits inside tens of thousands of donation flows. A new venture launches into that footprint, warmed by a 500+ partner network, instead of starting cold. Taken together, the portfolio already serves more than 8,000 clients.

Why it compounds

Every venture makes the next one easier.

We have built a portfolio of companies in which each strengthens the launch of the next: marketing, profitable client sourcing, SEO/GEO & nonprofit management. The infrastructure is in place.
