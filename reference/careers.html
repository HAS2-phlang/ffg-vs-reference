Why this team exists

This is a bench of investors, not an offshore support function.

Foundry for Good has spent fourteen years building seven profitable companies in the U.S. social good economy. The studio is the investment arm that funds the next ones, and it needs an investment and operating team of its own.

We are building that team in the Philippines. The people who join it will run diligence on live theses, sit in on portfolio operating reviews, work alongside U.S. company leadership on growth problems, and be first in line for a venture lead seat when a company is capitalized.

The studio's stated intent is to source venture leads from this bench. That is the career path: associate to operator to running a company you hold equity in.

What the seat looks like

Real portfolio work, from week one.

A small team means no layer between you and the companies. You will be in the operating conversations, not summarizing them.

Deal work

Live diligence

Market sizing, buyer interviews, competitive teardowns, and the memo that decides whether a thesis gets capital. Theses die at this gate, and you own the argument.

Portfolio work

Operating reviews

Bi-weekly check-ins with company leadership across seven operating brands: pipeline, retention, hiring, and the bottleneck of the month.

Build work

Inside a new venture

When a company is capitalized, someone from this bench is in it: standing up go-to-market, running the first customer conversations, hiring the first team.

Access

The partners, directly

The people who commit the capital operate the businesses funding it. You work with them, not with a reporting line to them.

Path

A venture lead seat

The studio sources operators from this bench. The end state is running a funded company with 15–45% equity, salaried from day one.

Platform

140+ specialists behind you

Engineering, design, growth, finance, and recruiting operate as shared functions across the portfolio. You pull on them the same way a founder does.

The process

How hiring works here.

The same diligence we run on an investment, applied to a person. It is deliberately specific about what you have actually done.

Step 01

Intro conversation

Twenty minutes on what you have run, what you own now, and what you want to own next.

Step 02

Working session

A real problem from the portfolio or a live thesis. We look at how you reason, not whether you land on our answer.

Step 03

Partner conversation

Time with the partners who commit the capital, and with an operator from one of the companies.

Step 04

Offer and start date

Complete terms in writing, including how the path to a venture lead seat works from where you are joining.

What we look for

  • A specific record: something you owned, and what happened to it.
  • Comfort with ambiguity, because most of the work is deciding what the question is.
  • Judgment about people, since we invest in operators as much as businesses.
  • Genuine interest in the social good economy, not just in venture as a career.
  • Willingness to be in the operating seat rather than adjacent to it.

What we do not require

  • A prior venture capital title. Operators convert well here.
  • A specific school or a specific city inside the Philippines.
  • Nonprofit sector experience on day one, though it accelerates you.
  • A finished idea. Theses come from the studio as often as from the individual.

Candidates in the Philippines: we are actively meeting people through finance and operator networks in Manila. If you would rather talk in person than apply by email, say so in your note.
