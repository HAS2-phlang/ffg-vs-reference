The investment arm of Foundry for Good.

Foundry for Good is investing and building the best mission-driven companies in the U.S. The venture studio is our channel to get there: we offer capital, an active operating role, a powerful network, and a proven, successful playbook behind every company we back.

WHO WE ARE

We are the investors and operators behind seven profitable companies in the social good economy, and the studio is how we build the next seven.

Foundry for Good has spent fourteen years starting, acquiring, and scaling companies that serve nonprofits, associations, and the organizations working on behalf of them. Double the Donation, Nexus Marketing, Getting Attention, Tradewing, NXUnite, eCardWidget, and Fuller Focus AI were all built or bought inside that period, and all of them are operating today.

Our venture studio is the investment arm. Companies reach out to us to fund, accelerate, or improve their mission-driven businesses. We capitalize our investments directly from the balance sheet of our successful operating businesses, staff them to scale with a 140+ person team in the U.S. & the Philippines, and re-invest towards their growth.

Our approach is uniquely active. We provide capital and manpower, and we connect the companies we back to their target audience with the help of our industry experts. We believe social good isn't achieved by sitting down. We actively support and mentor: the key ingredient behind our consistent success.

What we do

Our studio has 4 main mandates:

We research, invest, scale, and hold. We make sure the mission-driven companies gets the growth they need to scale into their potential. 

Mandate 01

Research

We source venture ideas or mission-driven companies that fit our investment thesis. We review the business's potential with our capital, network, playbooks, and go-to-market strategies. Then we reach out to the founders to learn more about the company. We want to make sure that the businesses have operated for at least five years and have held healthy margins for at least two.

Mandate 02

Invest

We buy mission-driven businesses at $250K–$5M in annual recurring revenue. We move at the speed of a founder: an offer in 7 days and most deals close inside a month. 

Mandate 03

Scale

We improve and scale the business in avenues it could not do alone. We expand its market reach via our large audience and network. We augment its go-to-market strategies, and provide value-add strategies from experienced operators. We  also offer sales strategies, back-office set-up, and hiring support once the business is ready. We measure our studio based on what our companies can do after the money lands.

Mandate 04

Hold

We hold and re-invest. We want to invest in your mission and in you.  Our companies have compounded inside our portfolio. We leave it to the founders on their options in the long-run. Our best interest is on how we help take your business to the next level.

How we differ

What this is, and what it is not.

Most people researching about our studio for the first time think we're a venture fund / a private equity firm: we're not.

We are Not a venture fund
We don't have a ten-year clock. We don't have the stringent requirements that are present via external funding.  We answer to the businesses and its needs to grow. We build with you from our own balance sheet and capacity. 
we are Not a private equity firm
Our capital is from our own balance sheet: not from debt nor any outside backing. We do not sacrifice the business for short-term gains and planned exits. We make sure that you are in a timeline that you need to scale your business sustainably: for good. 
we are Not passive investors
We know how important it is to be active equity holders. We invest our time, manpower, and expertise into making sure that you can grow given the capital that you have. We know what you need to succeed in this exact market and that's why we're inspired to help you every step of the way.
we are Not a services agency
Our motif of helping you with manpower, marketing, and strategy is part of our active investment approach. We have done this as it has consistently worked, and we'll keep doing it for a long time.

A venture studio that backs you and builds with you: what's in it for us is to see more companies succeed in the mission-driven space and create positive change within the space.
Inside the studio

How a company actually gets made.

The sequence below is the same for every venture. It is deliberately slow at the front and fast once capital is committed.

Step 01

Validate the idea

A problem surfaces from inside the portfolio or from a client conversation. We test it against the challenges specific to the nonprofit sector and pressure-test product-market fit before any leadership is onboarded. Most ideas are challenged here, which is the point.

Step 02

Find the two co-founders

Only once the market is proven do we go looking for people. We match two co-founders with complementary skills, one technical and one business, and run the same diligence on them that we run on any investment. They can be based in the U.S. or the Philippines.

Step 03

Capitalize the company

The company is incorporated with its own cap table. $250K lands, base salaries start from day one, and the venture is funded for roughly two years of runway. The founding team holds 15–45% between them.

Step 04

Put the platform behind it

Marketing and sales strategy, talent acquisition, back-office set-up, inbound marketing, and weekly mentorship from value-creation specialists. The venture launches into a 500+ partner network in the mission-driven sector instead of a cold start.

Step 05

Reach profitability

Getting Attention is the reference case: built inside the studio in 2023 and, eighteen months in, running roughly $21K in monthly recurring revenue, about 40 inbound leads a month, an NPS above 9, and profitable without outside investment, on four full-time people globally.

Step 06

Hold and compound

Profitable and independent, the owners choose: take distributions, position for a sale, or keep compounding. We do not have a fund clock the owners have optionality to choose what they want to do moving forward: and they often want to keep growing with us.

Track record

Fourteen years, seven companies.

We started with a companies that worked and have built upon their successes.

2011

Double the Donation is founded

Adam Weinger starts it after missing three of his own employer matching gift deadlines, with Kanwei Li joining as Chief Technology Officer. It is now used by 7,000+ nonprofits and schools and has identified over $1 billion in workplace giving opportunities for them.

Then

Nexus Marketing becomes the distribution layer

Founded by Rafi Norberg, it is the only digital marketing agency built around how mission-driven, community-impact, and social good professionals find the products, people, and services they need. Its 50+ specialists are the go-to-market engine the rest of the portfolio launches into.

2022

eCardWidget is backed

Tim Badolato had founded the eCard platform two years earlier and was running it alone. The studio invested in the existing business and put its marketing engine behind it: inbound strategy, SEO content, video, and partnership access, plus ongoing guidance on product roadmap and pricing. Badolato stayed CEO and still runs it today. The first company backed rather than built or bought.

2023

Getting Attention is built inside the studio

A digital marketing firm for nonprofits, incubated from a validated thesis and launched with zero outside investment. Eighteen months in it was profitable, at roughly $21K in monthly recurring revenue and about 40 inbound leads a month. The first company built end to end on the studio model.

Oct 2024

Tradewing is acquired

A membership platform for professional and trade associations with untapped potential. Three new departments follow: client success, sales, and marketing, along with faster feature rollouts and client retention up 28% after the acquisition.

Late 2025

Fuller Focus AI launches

An AI sales intelligence platform for teams selling into the nonprofit sector, giving them verified decision-maker data and organizational intelligence that CRMs and public databases do not carry. Founded and run by Alexander Bushe, who came into the studio as a Business Lead. The newest company in the portfolio and the first built on an AI-native thesis.

Today

Seven brands, fourteen years of consecutive growth

Double the Donation, Nexus Marketing, Getting Attention, Tradewing, NXUnite, eCardWidget, and Fuller Focus AI, supported by a 100+ person team in the Philippines with a 95% retention rate and every leadership seat filled from inside.

Who decides

Decisions are made by people who have run these companies.

There is no investment committee of outsiders. Capital is committed by the partners who operate the businesses funding it: Adam Weinger, who founded Double the Donation, and Rafi Norberg, who founded Nexus Marketing, with Kanwei Li on the technical side.

Around them sits an advisory bench drawn from across the nonprofit and association software economy: Salvatore Salpietro, Travis Rush, Steve Johns, and Jacob Fabbri on the angel network side, plus the operators running the portfolio itself, including Lomesh Shah at Tradewing, Jessica King at Getting Attention, Tim Badolato at eCardWidget, and Alexander Bushe at Fuller Focus AI. They are brought in on diligence, hiring, and go-to-market questions where their judgment is sharper than ours.
