How we pick ventures

We build businesses that matter.

We identify problems worth solving and ideas already in demand, run initial due diligence, debate, validate, and pressure-test against our experience in go-to-market, sales, and operations. Then we bring the idea to the table with the best-fit co-founder.

Idea Identification & Validation

Through years of experience, we've identified challenges unique to the nonprofit sector that new ventures can solve. Our initial validation and diligence ensures strong product-market fit before bringing venture leads onboard.

Founder Matching

We establish each business with a venture lead who brings the complementary skills: domain expertise, proven business acumen, and a known track record of growing companies.

We don't just hire, we tailor-fit according to you

The 2 venture lead types: Business Lead & Technical Lead.

Whether you're tenured on business savvy or you're experienced in leading the technical side of big tech. We want to work with you.

Business & Operations Lead

You run the company

You own go-to-market, customers, hiring, and the P&L from day one. You have the experience as an executive or senior manager running a team or a function before, and you would rather own a business, rather than work for one. Direct corporate leadership, entrepreneurial leadership, or nonprofit & association experience is a strong signal, because you have experience on what needs to get done.

Technical Lead

You build the product

You own product and engineering from the first line of code and set the technical direction as the company grows. You've shipped real products for someone else; now you want to own one, with the equity to match.
