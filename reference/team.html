The people behind the capital.

This is the investment team and advisory bench behind the vehicle: experienced operators who back every venture with capital, judgment, and a network built over fourteen years.

Your vision: backed by investors and advisors

Adam Weinger

PARTNER

Founder & President of Double the Donation · CEO, Foundry for Good

Adam founded Double the Donation in 2011 after his own employer's matching gift deadline lapsed on donations he had made to his university and to the Leukemia & Lymphoma Society. The platform he built to fix that problem now serves more than 7,000 nonprofits and schools, tracks over 26,000 workplace giving programs, and integrates with 100+ fundraising platforms. Adam also serves as Chief Executive Officer of Foundry for Good, the investment vehicle built on that foundation. He graduated from Emory University's Goizueta Business School with a BBA in Finance and Economics and Mathematics.

Rafi Norberg

PARTNER

President of Nexus Marketing

Rafi founded Nexus Marketing in 2014, building it from a side project during his consulting years at Deloitte into an agency of more than 50 specialists serving nonprofits, associations, and the companies behind them. He has spent over a decade defining how mission-driven brands grow through search and content, working with more than 200 organizations across the sector, and created NXUnite, the largest open community network for nonprofit professionals. Rafi co-founded Foundry for Good alongside Adam Weinger. He graduated from Emory University.

Kanwei Li

PARTNER

Chief Technology Officer

Kanwei is the co-founder and Chief Technology Officer of Double the Donation, where he serves as lead architect of the platform behind the industry's most widely embedded matching gift technology. His open-source work dates to Google Summer of Code in 2008, and his algorithms and data structures library remains one of the most widely used of its kind. Before Double the Donation, he was a senior software engineer in Atlanta's technology sector. Kanwei holds both a BS and an MS in Computer Science from Emory University.

Salvatore Salpietro

ADVISOR

Chief Growth Officer, Dataro

Salvatore is Chief Growth Officer at Dataro, the AI fundraising intelligence platform, which he joined as its first non-founder executive alongside its $14.28M Series A. Before Dataro, he was a founding employee and Chief Community Officer at Fundraise Up, one of the fastest-growing donation platforms in the sector. Earlier, as Director of Digital at the Child Mind Institute, he led a digital overhaul that grew online donation revenue fivefold, and he co-founded Icebox, a digital agency whose clients included Procter & Gamble, Nike, and the Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation.

Travis Rush

ADVISOR

Chief Executive Officer, Kellen

Travis is Chief Executive Officer of Kellen, the association management firm founded in 1964, where he oversees programming for more than eighty client organizations. He rose through Kellen from business development to Chief Operating Officer to the chief executive seat, and led the firm's headquarters relocation to the National Press Building in Washington, DC, anchoring one of the largest concentrations of association headquarters in the capital. Before Kellen, his career spanned real estate, film production, consulting, and sales. Travis graduated from George Washington University.

Steve Johns

ADVISOR

CEO Boardable, Former CEO of OneCause

Steve led OneCause as Chief Executive Officer for over a decade, growing the fundraising technology company from its BidPal roots into a platform used by more than 10,000 nonprofits that has powered over $5 billion in giving, through to its acquisition by Bonterra in 2025. Earlier in his career he was a Director of Financial Advisory Services at PwC and Vice President of Global Business Development at Gateway. He serves on the board of Boardable and is the author of Fearless: Leadership Lessons at the Crossroads.

Jacob Fabbri

ADVISOR

Chief Marketing Officer, FinQuery

Jacob is Chief Marketing Officer at FinQuery, the Atlanta-based accounting technology company. Before FinQuery he spent five years as Chief Marketing Officer of Togetherwork, leading marketing across its portfolio of community management software, after a decade at Fonteva, the Salesforce-native association platform, where he rose to Chief Marketing Officer and his team helped drive over 1,000% growth in under five years. He was named one of the DC region's top CMOs in 2018 and brings more than 20 years of B2B technology marketing experience in the association and nonprofit sector.

Ryan Gaines

OPERATIONS

Chief Financial Officer - Foundry for Good

Ryan is a Partner and Chief Financial Officer of Foundry for Good, where he oversees financial strategy across the portfolio. He is also the founder of N6 Finance, a fractional CFO practice that helps venture-backed startups from pre-seed through Series B raise capital, automate finance, and build systems that scale. Ryan began his career as an investment banking analyst at Lazard and an investment analyst at Trian Fund Management. He graduated from Emory University's Goizueta Business School with a BBA in Finance, Accounting, and Alternative Investments.
