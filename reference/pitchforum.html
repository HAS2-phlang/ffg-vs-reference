The Pitch Forum
for companies that sell to nonprofits.

The Pitch Forum is where companies that sell to nonprofits, schools and associations present their business to a small group of industry operators, who have the option to invest. It is run by Foundry for Good, a specialized group of investors that invests in companies serving the mission-driven sector, and every company that presents receives a written answer within 48 hours.

The pitch experience

  • Who you're talking to: our panel is made up of industry experts and operators in the space. They have built, run, and successfully sold a company that serves nonprofits, schools, or associations. They're excited to see, weigh, and support your business.
  • How it runs: we host these sessions quarterly via Zoom or Google Meet. You'll be scheduled your own dedicated time slot to pitch and we'll give you our feedback.
  • What comes next: after your session, an investor might offer to fund you, partner with you, or mentor you to help prepare for a later round.

What happens if we invest

If we decide to partner up, we bring a lot more to the table than just capital to help you grow:

  • Context and knowledge: deep expertise on how to effectively build for and sell to the nonprofit sector.
  • The right network: direct introductions to specialized partners, nonprofits, and resources tailored to this market.
  • Targeted reach: access to niche marketing channels that put your product directly in front of nonprofits.
  • Back-office support: centralized operations for hiring, finance, and accounting, all provided at cost so you can focus on building your business.
Eligibility

Who can apply.

Six things we look for. You don't need all six to be perfect, but we'll ask about each one.

Market

You're a B2B company selling to nonprofits, schools, associations, foundations or social enterprises.

Traction

A working product with paying customers, or completed pilots with results.

Growth stalled

Proven product, but growth's gone flat? We look at those too.

Measurable results

Pilot outcomes, customer results or revenue. A forecast on its own won't do it.

Commercial viability

A model that can grow inside this market, and that depends on serving it.

A defined request

How much you're raising, who you want to meet, and what the money is for.

Session structure

How each session runs.

It's the same for every company, and it's the format Rafi and Adam already run.

STEP 01 · 10 MINUTES

Your pitch

Six slides: problem, solution, traction, team, gaps, the ask. Nobody interrupts.

STEP 02 · 10 MINUTES

Q&A

The angels ask you directly. A moderator keeps the clock.

STEP 03 · 5 MINUTES

We deliberate

You drop off the call. Each angel fills in a short scorecard on your pitch.

STEP 04 · WITHIN 48 HOURS

We get back to you

In writing, with where you landed and why.

The scorecard asks each angel four things: how big the problem is, how strong the product is, what the traction shows, and whether the team can pull it off. Then one more: Pass, Mentor, or Investigate.

If two or more angels pick Investigate, we set up a deeper diligence call with you. If they pick Mentor, we introduce you for a short advisory conversation. Participation in any deal is fully optional, on both sides.
